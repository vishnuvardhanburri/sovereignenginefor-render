# One-Day Close Room

Objective: drive toward £500,000 in urgent pipeline by prioritizing buyers who can close white-label licenses, acquisition conversations, or multiple deployment rights quickly.

Commercial source of truth:

- Internal Enterprise License: £40,000 GBP
- White-Label Commercial License: £160,000 GBP
- Operations & Maintenance: £3,000 GBP/month

## Today Only Priority

Do not optimize for replies. Optimize for qualified money movement.

Priority order:

1. Agencies, RevOps firms, deliverability consultants, and AI automation operators that can monetize the £160,000 white-label license.
2. Strategic buyers who ask about acquisition, source code, or commercial rights at £200K+.
3. SaaS or cybersecurity teams already running outbound internally at £40,000.

## £500K Fast Math

Fastest credible paths:

- 3 white-label commercial licenses = £480,000 plus first maintenance month = £489,000.
- 2 white-label commercial licenses + 1 acquisition/license conversation at £200K = £520,000.
- 1 white-label commercial license + 1 strategic acquisition/license at £320K + maintenance = £483,000, then close one £40,000 internal license to pass £500,000.

Therefore the daily motion should prioritize white-label and strategic buyers first. Internal £40,000 licenses are useful, but they are not the main path to £500,000.

## Closeable Offers

### Fast Internal Close

Use when the buyer wants internal operating control.

Ask:

```text

```

### White-Label Close

Use when the buyer has clients or wants resale rights.

Ask:

```text
For client deployment, resale, or rebrand rights, the correct license is the £160,000 GBP white-label commercial license. If that is the direction, I can send the commercial rights summary and invoice today.
```

### Diligence Deposit

Use when they are serious but cannot complete the full license inside the day.

Ask:

```text
To lock the codebase walkthrough, data room, and founder support while you review, we can take a diligence deposit today and credit it toward the £40,000 internal or £160,000 white-label license.
```

## Qualification Gate

Do not send the repo or data room until they answer at least two:

- Internal use or white-label use?
- Who signs off on this purchase?
- Can they move this week if the walkthrough fits?
- Are they already running outbound or serving outbound/growth clients?
- Do they want source-code handoff, managed deployment, or commercial rights?

## Call Flow

1. Confirm use case: internal or white-label.
2. Show sent proof and health dashboard first.
3. Show the two price paths.
4. Ask for close:

```text
Based on what you saw, is this an internal £40,000 license conversation or a £160,000 white-label rights conversation?
```

5. If yes, move to invoice/license.
6. If not today, ask for diligence deposit.
